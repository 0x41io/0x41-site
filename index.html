01 / recon

mønømi

Passive attack surface recon, explained. Monomi maps a domain's public footprint from certificate logs and public data, then Claude explains what it means: what's exposed, what deserves a closer look, and what to fix. Every finding cites numbered evidence, so you can check it yourself.

evidence

Findings are labeled Observed, Inference or Lead, and every one cites its source and collection time.

passive

Reads public data by default and never scans the target. The one active check is opt-in.

checks

Subdomains, hosting, open ports and CVEs, email spoofing, subdomain takeover and security headers.

mcp

Run it as an MCP server so AI agents like Claude can call its checks directly.

ip intel

Check suspicious IPs for Tor, VPN, hosting or CDN edge. Every label names its source.

kaidoku

Hands every CVE it finds straight to kaidøku for triage.

02 / triage

kaidøku

CVE triage in plain English. Pulls data from NVD and CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, ranks what matters for your stack, and explains exploitability and business impact.

batch

Paste a list or a scan export and get a ranked summary table.

kev

Flags CVEs used in known ransomware campaigns, straight from CISA.

profiles

Describe your stack once; in-stack CVEs rank first.
